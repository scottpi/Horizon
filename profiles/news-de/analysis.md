# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the importance of general news — politics, government, international affairs, society, public safety, and domestic events — for a general reader who wants to stay informed about what's happening in Germany and the world.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Landmark.** Major political decisions, historic events, significant international conflicts or agreements, or developments with broad, lasting consequences for many people.
- **7-8: Important.** Significant policy decisions, notable political developments, major incidents, or international events with a clear and substantial effect on public life.
- **5-6: Noteworthy.** Solid, credible news worth knowing, moderate scope or consequence, or developments that add meaningful context to an ongoing situation.
- **3-4: Routine.** Minor political statements, local news of limited scope, or incremental updates to an already-known situation.
- **0-2: Noise.** Trivial items, celebrity gossip, unverified rumors, or purely promotional content.

# Evaluation guidance

Consider the scope of impact (how many people are affected, and how directly), the credibility and specificity of the source, and whether the item adds new, concrete information rather than repeating known facts or offering pure speculation. Prefer items with clear factual substance over opinion pieces. Do not reward sensational headlines that overstate the actual event.

Use three to five specific topic tags.
