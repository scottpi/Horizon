# German general news profile

Use this profile for general news about politics, government, international affairs, society, public safety, and domestic events — the kind of coverage a general daily news broadcast or newspaper front page leads with.

Typical items include government decisions and policy debates, elections, international relations and conflicts, court rulings, public safety incidents, and significant social or societal developments.

Do not use this profile for items whose primary content is technology, software, AI/ML, or computing (use the technology profile), nor for items primarily about markets, company earnings, business deals, or economic data (use the financial news profile) — even when a general-news item mentions technology or the economy in passing.
