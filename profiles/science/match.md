# Science / Research profile

Use this profile for research findings and science journalism in physics, astronomy, biology, chemistry, earth science, medicine, materials science, mathematics, and other natural or formal sciences.

Typical items include peer-reviewed study results, credible science journalism explaining new findings, major research institution announcements, and space/astronomy discoveries.

Do not use this profile for computing, software, or AI/ML research — even when published by a research institution — that belongs in the technology profile instead. Do not use this profile for pure opinion pieces, general-audience explainers of settled science with no new finding, or promotional institutional press releases without a genuine result.
